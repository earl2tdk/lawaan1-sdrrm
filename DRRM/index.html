CLASS CONTINUITY PROTOCOL

The 4H Protocol System

Action guidelines governing class suspension and learning modalities during weather events.

HAYO

Continue

Normal in-person school operations proceed safely.

HINAY

Slow Down

Minor disruptions; modular or online learning is engaged.

HINGA

Check-in

Inclement conditions; wellbeing check-ins without heavy academic burden.

HINTO

Stop

Critical weather hazards; all classes and activities are suspended.
